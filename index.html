Bad Bluetooth

BitPool Demo

The bitpool feature works by changing the number of bits available per subband. This value is normally agreed upon by the transmittor and receiver when the connection is first established and then remains unchanged for the duration of the connection. The number of available bits are distributed using a signal to noise style method. This means the frequency band with the most energy is allocated the most bits and the less energy a band contains the less bits it will be allocated. At its extreme there are only two bits available which produces an interesting distortion effect.
